# "Get animation info" for a sequence is split across two namespaces with two verb names — the intuitive `animation.get_animation_info` guess UNKNOWN_ACTIONs

The common intent "read a `UAnimSequence` so I know its length / frame count" maps to two *different* methods that share neither namespace nor verb:

- **`animation.describe_sequence`** — the top-level dump-parity live read (delegates to `AnimSequenceDumpBuilder`, returns the full `anim_sequence.json` shape). This is what a caller actually wants for "tell me this clip's length."
- **`animation.authoring.get_animation_info`** — the authoring-layer "introspection dual" (a thinner, different shape), under the *sub*-namespace.

So the verb a caller naturally reaches for, `get_animation_info`, lives only under `animation.authoring`, while the top-level reader uses a *different* verb, `describe_sequence`. The most intuitive composition for "get info about an animation in the top-level animation namespace" — `animation.get_animation_info` — resolves to **neither** method and returns `[UNKNOWN_ACTION] Unknown action: animation.get_animation_info`. The caller must then wiki-navigate to discover that the top-level reader is spelled `describe_sequence`.

The top-level `animation.md` "How to use" bullet (line 9) does mention `animation.describe_sequence` ("the dump-parity live read for `UAnimSequence`"), but it is buried mid-sentence inside a long convenience-method list, and there is no signpost telling a reader who guessed `get_animation_info` that (a) the verb is `describe_sequence` at top level, and (b) the `get_animation_info` verb they reached for is the `animation.authoring` one. Nothing aliases or cross-references the two info readers, so the natural first guess is a dead end.

This is a discoverability/naming gap, not a tool bug: once the caller lands on `animation.describe_sequence` it works perfectly (the task read both `Dino_Idle` and `Dino_Walk` as 0.967s / 30f). The cost is the wasted misuse-then-correct round trip on the wrong verb before discovery.

**Workaround:** for a sequence's length/info use `animation.describe_sequence` (top-level, dump-parity); `get_animation_info` is the `animation.authoring`-namespaced introspection dual, not a top-level verb.

**Fix (docs):** In `docs/wiki-src/animation.md`, add an explicit signpost for the "read a sequence" intent — name `animation.describe_sequence` as the go-to length/info reader (pull it out of the mid-sentence convenience list into its own line or callout), and cross-reference that the `get_animation_info` verb lives under `animation.authoring` (with a one-line note on how the two readers differ: `describe_sequence` = full dump-parity shape, `get_animation_info` = authoring introspection dual). Mirror the cross-reference from `animation.authoring.md`'s Inspect-after-mutate section (line 18) so a reader on either page is pointed at the other info reader. This is the same "obvious-verb-guess misses" discoverability class as `E-asset-search-vs-search-assets-overlap` (no `asset.find`) and `E-viewport-screenshot-name-discovery`, here for the split animation-sequence info readers.
